BAKE - one clean cook of a map, split into COMPILE and SERIALIZE.
COMPILE   = cache-free CsgWorld.Build (the overload ScmapBake calls: no previous world, no carve cache,
            because a bake must be a pure function of its source) plus BspFlattener.Flatten per cell.
SERIALIZE = ScmapBuilder staging (AddNode per brush, AddChunk per cell) plus Build, which emits
            STRT/ASTB/META/NODE/CHDR/CMSH/CBSP. The bake's own glue is counted HERE deliberately: it is
            bookkeeping paid only because a file is being written, and putting it on this side can only
            make the serializer look worse than it is.
NEITHER HALF IS INFERRED BY SUBTRACTION. The compile's own cost swings by two orders of magnitude across
these content sets, so a serialize time taken as total-minus-compile would be flattered by exactly the
content that compiles slowest. A clean cook is legitimately O(world) and nothing here argues otherwise -
the incremental story belongs to the cook cache and its own no-op-cook test. What this guards is the SHARE.
